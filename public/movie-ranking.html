This is my fav movie just bcs of its bgm, eventhough i have watched movies with better storyline than this but this breaks the mark just bcs of the BGM. If you are a fan of romcoms you should give it a shot 100%.

2) How To Lose A Guy In 10 Days -

3) Crazy, Stupid, Love -

This movie is the perfect mix of romance and comedy a gold romcom picture if you want a laugh must watch it.
